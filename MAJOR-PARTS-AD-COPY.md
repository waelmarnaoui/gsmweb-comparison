# Display, battery and charging-port advertising copy

The owner requested that only displays, batteries and charging ports contribute to stock-based advertising. The adapter keeps received-stock history intact, but advertising qualification accepts only canonical part types display, battery and charging-port (case normalized). Other small parts, speakers, cameras, adhesives and unrelated categories are excluded from draft preparation, the advertising parts table and new small-part stock alerts. No product-name heuristics invent a category.

Every newly generated draft and each of its three copy variants now contains exactly 15 distinct headline assets and four descriptions. Three headline assets use real Google keyword insertion:

- {KeyWord:Inlocuire Display}
- GSMWEB {KeyWord:Display}
- {KeyWord:Display} Sector 6

The defaults change by the verified part type. Other headlines use recorded compatible models, the verified business name, Drumul Taberei 138, Sector 6, in-store replacement/installation and contact/evaluation language. No new price, delivery-time, warranty, original-part, dealer or manufacturer-affiliation claim is added. Very long model labels are omitted from headline slots rather than truncated into invented model names; the verified keyword rules still apply.

The validator checks the displayed fallback against the 30-character headline limit and descriptions against 90 characters. The insertion preview inserts a reviewed keyword only if the complete rendered headline fits; otherwise it shows the default. Curly braces around arbitrary text are not keyword insertion. Dynamic insertion does not narrow location targeting or guarantee lower CPC, approval or performance.

Generated headlines, descriptions and keywords exclude service gsm and Romanian reparatie/reparatii word forms, regardless of case, punctuation or diacritics. This is a copy preference, not an attempt to hide the service: assets still explicitly describe replacement and in-store installation. The business-offer policy review remains intact. Existing live ads and live keywords are not changed by this source update.

Insertion uses the triggering ad-group keyword, not arbitrary search-query text. Suggested keywords use only the allowed part family and recorded compatible models, such as inlocuire display Samsung Galaxy S23. A target ad group must be audited before submission, or existing keywords could dynamically insert restricted or irrelevant text. The internal policy review records this requirement; no Google ad submission is added here.

New snapshots carry a copy-rules version. Historical snapshots are retained, but old draft sets are hidden on the current preparation screen until the next successful five-minute stock collection generates the new assets. The main agent counts only the allowed stock families and current-format draft sets. No new credentials, environment settings, migrations or live Ads changes are required.
